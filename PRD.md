# Team Crimson PRD ( BU-ride )
After concluding research Team Crrimson discovered a problem common to some students who find it hard to move around in school, although the school already made transportation within the school easier by introducing tricycles(keke) but at the same time it's not all students that are looking for a ride that get a ride.

## Objectives 
Our team is creating a software that will help organize the transportation system and help student and probably teachers get transportation from anywhere in the school to even outside the school. This software will also be accesssible to commercial drivers in the area who may be available. This will all be accessible from phones making it as covenient as possible.

## Target users
- Students
- Teachers
- Tricycle owners 
- Commercial drivers, e.t.c

## Functional Requirements

1. **User Registration and Login:** The system must allow students, drivers, and administrators to register and log in securely.

2. **Ride Booking:** Students must be able to request transportation from one location within the school.

3. **Driver Assignment:** The system must assign available drivers to students' transportation requests.



4. **Ride Status Updates:** The system must display ride statuses such as Pending, Accepted, In Progress, and Completed.

5. **Fare Management:** The system must calculate transportation fares based on distance or predefined school transportation rates.


6. **Driver Management:** Administrators must be able to add, edit, suspend, and remove driver accounts.

7. **Vehicle Management:** Administrators must be able to register vehicles and manage their availability and details.

8. **Ride History:** Students must be able to view their previous transportation requests and completed rides.

9. **Notifications:** The system must notify students and drivers about booking confirmations, ride assignments, and important updates.

10. **Admin Dashboard:** Administrators must be able to monitor transportation activities, manage users, and view ride reports.

11. **Ride Cancellation:** Students must be able to cancel a ride request before the journey begins, subject to school policies.

12. **Feedback and Ratings:** Students must be able to provide feedback about their transportation experience.

## Non-Functional Requirements

1. **Performance:** The system should respond to user requests within 3 seconds under normal operating conditions.

2. **Security:** The system must protect user accounts and sensitive information through secure authentication and encryption.

3. **Reliability:** The system should remain operational and provide consistent transportation services with minimal downtime.

4. **Usability:** The application should have a simple, user-friendly interface that students, drivers, and administrators can easily navigate.

5. **Scalability:** The system should support an increasing number of students, drivers, vehicles, and transportation requests.

6. **Availability:** The system should be accessible whenever transportation services are operating.

7. **Data Privacy:** Students' personal information and real-time location data must only be accessible to authorized users.

8. **Compatibility:** The application should work on commonly used Android and iOS devices.

9. **Maintainability:** The system should be designed in a way that allows developers to fix errors, improve features, and perform updates easily.

10. **Accuracy:** The system should provide accurate vehicle locations, ride information, and fare calculations.

| Driver | As a driver, I want to receive ride requests so that I can transport students. |
